5. ADR Assessment

The reported symptoms are suspected to be associated with amlodipine because they developed after initiation of the medication.

Dizziness and headache may occur following antihypertensive treatment, particularly when blood pressure decreases after starting or increasing therapy. Peripheral edema (ankle swelling) is a recognized adverse effect associated with amlodipine.

The temporal relationship between initiation of amlodipine and development of symptoms supports a possible drug-related reaction. However, other causes should also be considered.

6. Suspected Drug

Amlodipine

7. Suspected ADR

Possible amlodipine-associated dizziness, headache, fatigue, and peripheral edema.

8. Causality Assessment

Based on the available information, the reaction can be considered possible/probable.

Additional information would be required for a more complete causality assessment, including:

Baseline blood pressure
Blood pressure after starting amlodipine
Previous history of similar symptoms
Other antihypertensive medications
Relevant medical conditions
Dechallenge information
Alternative causes
9. Conclusion

Amlodipine is the suspected drug associated with the reported adverse reactions based on the temporal relationship between drug initiation and symptom development. The occurrence of ankle swelling is particularly consistent with a known adverse effect of amlodipine. Further clinical information would be required to establish causality more confidently.
